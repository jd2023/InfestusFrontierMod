# Sprint S04: a self-supplying Overworld

Milestone: M3. Status: in progress.

Goal: biomass, water and items move on their own between organs; a farm feeds the Sac and keeps its seed; one store grows; a reserve holds; worn armor refuels at base.

Acceptance (ROADMAP.md, M3): three complete reference supply cycles with a positive measured surplus; seed/water/restart reserves retained; 3D branches/crossings and port direction inspected visually; full outputs and unloaded routes cannot spill or duplicate.

Decisions, so the features do not reopen them:

- **F1.** The Activation Cyst is a batch organ kind: 1 Organ Bud + 2 Membrane Sheets + 1 Bone Plate; 3 slots, 1000 mB water, 1000 BU; its `organ_batch` recipes (`recipe/activating/`) take 400 ticks. Treatments are plain items, each owned by the module that applies it (the intermediates by `processing`), made per ITEM_CATALOG: Fusion Binder, Elastic Gel and Rooting Gel have a Bowl recipe at 1200 ticks and a Cyst recipe at 400; Capillary Gel, Contractile Fiber, Filter Membrane, Sealing Resin and Synaptic Gel have a Cyst recipe only. The guide gets a second category, Circulation. A treatment used on exposed mature Living Substrate turns the cell into its function block; its owner module registers that.
- **F2.** A vein is a block that replaces one mature substrate cell and stays an organ foundation. Three kinds: Biomass Vein (Capillary Gel), Fluid Vein (Filter Membrane, then Sealing Resin on the same cell), Item Vein (Contractile Fiber). A cell holds one packet: 250 mB, or 8 items of one kind. It never pulls. It receives through its capability, remembers the side it came from, and pushes the packet on a scheduled tick every 4 ticks (items 8): first into consumers on its other sides (up, down, north, east, south, west), then into the next vein. A full downstream leaves the packet where it is; nothing retries every tick. A broken vein carries its fluid on its item and drops its items. The Vascular Junction (1 Organ Bud + 2 Membrane Sheets + 1 slime ball) is the pump: one 1000 mB tank locked to the fluid it holds, six ports (closed, intake, outlet; the probe cycles the face it clicks), every 4 ticks it drains up to 250 mB from each intake neighbour (a water source block counts, by the vanilla bucket pickup) and fills each outlet neighbour. No pumping cost yet.
- **F3.** Mouths attach to a host block and face a partner: the Intake Mouth (Bud + hopper + Sheet) moves up to 8 items every 8 ticks from the partner's item handler into the host's; the Output Mouth (Bud + hopper + bone) the other way, taking only what the host offers for extraction. No filters yet. General storage follows BLOCK_CATALOG T1-09: vanilla containers and optional Storage Drawers.
  - Bounds: 64 admitted mouth pulses per dimension per tick, two loaded inventory endpoints, at most 16 source slots and one window of 16 destination slots per pulse. Unsuccessful scans continue over later pulses; successful slots continue at the eight-tick cadence. A full or missing endpoint retries after 20 ticks; quota denial retries after 21 to separate it from the idle cadence. One saved packet of at most eight items holds any insertion remainder. No chunk tickets, loose transfer items or network scan. A removed mouth drops that packet once.
- **F4.** Seed Pouch (leather + Sheet + Culture): four stacks of planting stock, a per-type reserve the stick sets, hoppers take only the surplus. Cultivation Tissue: hoe mature substrate, then Rooting Gel; vanilla crops grow on it as on farmland, no water block needed while a Fluid Vein or bucket keeps its 1000 mB tank wet. Planting Proboscis (Bud + wooden hoe + Rooting Gel + Sheet) plants from an adjacent pouch or its slot into the empty tissue of its 3x3 plot for 5 BU. Harvest Corolla (Bud + iron hoe + 2 Sheets) cuts the mature crops of its 3x3 plot for 5 BU each into its output slot; an Output Mouth or hopper takes them. Compost Gland is a batch organ kind: 4 seeds or 4 leaves + 100 mB water → 1 bone meal in 400 ticks.
- **F5.** A Biomass Reservoir Cell (Bladder + 2 Bone Plates + 2 Membrane Sheets) holds 4000 BU; touching cells in one rectangular body share one level; an incomplete row waits outside the body with its own content.
- **F6.** Sensor Polyp (Bud + comparator + spider eye): a comparator-style signal 0..15 for the fullness of the block it faces. Filter Valve (Bone Plate + slime ball + paper, on a vein): passes a packet only while the source behind it keeps the reserve the stick sets.
- **F7.** Fuel Papilla (Capillary Gel + Membrane Sheet on mature substrate): a 1000 BU cell fed by veins; a player standing on it has worn awakened pieces filled 10 BU per second each.
- **F8.** Septum Crossing (Sheet + Bone Plate on a vein): two passages on two axes, no mixing. Vascular Stitch (Contractile Fiber + Sheet): joins two veins across a one-block step.
- **F9.** One GameTest runs wheat from a farm through the Sac into a reservoir and back to the Bowl for three cycles and asserts the surplus; demo row A exhibits every block; captures show a branch, a crossing and port direction.
