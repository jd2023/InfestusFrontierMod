# Sprint S04: a self-supplying Overworld

Milestone: M3. Status: in progress.

Goal: biomass, water and items move on their own between organs; a farm feeds the Sac and keeps its seed; one store grows; a reserve holds; worn armor refuels at base.

Acceptance (ROADMAP.md, M3): three complete reference supply cycles with a positive measured surplus; seed/water/restart reserves retained; 3D branches/crossings and port direction inspected visually; full outputs and unloaded routes cannot spill or duplicate.

Decisions, so the features do not reopen them:

- **F1.** The Activation Cyst is a batch organ kind: 1 Organ Bud + 2 Membrane Sheets + 1 Bone Plate; 3 slots, 1000 mB water, 1000 BU; its `organ_batch` recipes (`recipe/activating/`) take 400 ticks. Treatments are plain items, each owned by the module that applies it (the intermediates by `processing`), made per ITEM_CATALOG: Fusion Binder, Elastic Gel and Rooting Gel have a Bowl recipe at 1200 ticks and a Cyst recipe at 400; Capillary Gel, Contractile Fiber, Filter Membrane, Sealing Resin and Synaptic Gel have a Cyst recipe only. The guide gets a second category, Circulation. A treatment used on exposed mature Living Substrate turns the cell into its function block; its owner module registers that.
- **F2.** A vein is a block that replaces one mature substrate cell and stays an organ foundation. Three kinds: Biomass Vein (Capillary Gel), Fluid Vein (Filter Membrane, then Sealing Resin on the same cell), Item Vein (Contractile Fiber). A cell holds one packet: 250 mB, or 8 items of one kind. It never pulls. It receives through its capability, remembers the side it came from, and pushes the packet on a scheduled tick every 4 ticks (items 8): first into consumers on its other sides (up, down, north, east, south, west), then into the next vein. A full downstream leaves the packet where it is; nothing retries every tick. The Vascular Junction (1 Organ Bud + 2 Membrane Sheets + 1 slime ball) is the pump: one 1000 mB tank locked to the fluid it holds, six ports (closed, intake, outlet; the probe cycles the face it clicks), every 4 ticks it drains up to 250 mB from each intake neighbour (a water source block counts, by the vanilla bucket pickup) and fills each outlet neighbour. No pumping cost yet.
- **F2b.** A vein cell links to cells of the same carrier on all six sides. The model shows a groove arm on the top face for each horizontal link, a lumen up the exposed side faces for a vertical link, and an opening in the top for a link up. The lumen texture flows (a vanilla animated texture). A fluid packet never goes back the way it came; a cell with several onward links serves them in turn, one per pulse.
- **F3.** A Mouth (1 Organ Bud + 1 hopper + 1 Membrane Sheet) is a cell of mature substrate with lips on its top face; it stays an organ foundation. Its partner is the inventory standing on it; its network is the Item Veins and Mouths joined to it face to face. Settings, on its screen: intake, output, both (for an organ: ingredients in, products out) or closed; nine filter slots as an allow or deny list; an amount (intake fills the partner up to it, output keeps it in the partner, 0 means none); a priority from -4 to 4 for intake. Any hand on the Mouth opens the screen; the probe on its partner does too.
  - Delivery: every 8 ticks a Mouth that gives offers its partner's items, 16 slots a pulse, to the network's Mouths that take: highest priority, then nearest, then in turn. It extracts up to 8 items only after a destination accepted them in a simulated insert, and keeps up to 4 such packets saved while they travel, 2 ticks per cell of the route; on arrival it re-checks the route and inserts. A refused remainder goes to the next accepting destination, or waits in its packet and retries every 20 ticks. Item Veins hold nothing and have no block entity.
  - Bounds: a network is at most 256 cells in loaded chunks, found by a breadth-first search that stops there; an output Mouth keeps its found destinations until a vein or Mouth in the dimension changes. 64 Mouth pulses per dimension per tick. No chunk tickets, loose items or per-tick retries. A broken Mouth drops its packet once.
  - Stores: vanilla containers and other mods' inventories through the standard item handler; the Item Capsule (F3b) natively.
- **F3c.** Owner, 2026-10-20: organ automation is a multiblock. A Feeding Lobe (Bud + Sheet + slime ball) and a Yield Lobe (Bud + Sheet + Bone Plate) stand on a foundation beside an organ and face it: a Feeding Lobe serves organs in `feeding_hosts`, a Yield Lobe organs in `yield_hosts` (not the Sac or the Planting Proboscis, whose stock is no product; the Harvest Corolla takes nothing in). The Feeding Lobe only inserts into the organ's own item handler, the Yield Lobe only extracts from it; a Mouth under the lobe, a pipe, or a hopper above a Feeding Lobe works it. The lobe's item handler is live: each call finds the organ again, so a pipe's cached handler never goes stale; placing or removing a lobe invalidates its capability. An organ serves 1 + its `stage` lobes of each kind, counted north, east, south, west; others are idle. A lobe has no block entity; it reads four loaded neighbours at most and never an unloaded chunk. Bare organs still take hoppers and pipes.
- **F3b.** The Item Capsule (1 chest + 2 Membrane Sheets + 1 Spore Culture) holds 4 item types, 16 stacks of each: 1024 of a kind that stacks to 64 (`core.storage.Compartments`). It needs no foundation and faces the player who places it. Touching capsules in one rectangular body form one store by the Reservoir's rule, the body's life shared in `storage.Bodies`; an incomplete row waits outside it with its own contents. Each cell keeps its own compartments and carries them on its item when broken; a filled cell does not stack, fits in no container item and goes into no capsule. The front of each cell shows each compartment's icon and count. Its standard item handler is the whole body, slot by slot, for hoppers and other mods: items join their kind's compartments before they open the first empty one, a slot gives at most a stack, and a body with a cell out of reach has no slots. Mouths count and insert through `storage.api.ItemStore`, the whole body at once; the Sensor Polyp reads its fullness from it. Any hand opens the whole body in pages of 16 compartments; shift-click inserts into the whole store. Put down carried items before paging.
- **F4.** Seed Pouch (leather + Sheet + Culture): four stacks of planting stock, one reserve for each kind that a stick raises by one, from 64 back to 0 (a sneaking hand builds, so nothing lowers), hoppers take only the surplus. Cultivation Tissue: Rooting Gel on exposed mature substrate (the catalog's hoe step is dropped: one graft like the veins); vanilla crops grow on it as on farmland, no water block needed while a Fluid Vein or bucket keeps its 1000 mB tank wet. Planting Proboscis (Bud + wooden hoe + Rooting Gel + Sheet) plants from its slot or any item handler beside it (a pouch gives only above its reserve) into the empty tissue of its 3x3 plot for 5 BU; the plot is the nine ground cells under and around the organ. Harvest Corolla (Bud + iron hoe + 2 Sheets) cuts the mature crops of its 3x3 plot for 5 BU each into its output slot; a Mouth set to output or a hopper takes them. Compost Gland is a batch organ kind: 4 seeds or 4 leaves + 100 mB water → 1 bone meal in 400 ticks.
- **F5.** A Biomass Reservoir Cell (Bladder + 2 Bone Plates + 2 Membrane Sheets; the cell keeps the Bladder's biomass) holds 4000 BU and stands on a foundation, another cell or a Vascular Junction. The body rule is `core.storage.Body`, shared with the Item Capsule: touching cells join only when the union is a full box, up to 4 on a side, that splits no other body; a cell that would leave a gap waits outside with its own content, and the existing body keeps priority, so the order of placement decides. Each cell holds its own share and saves its body's box; the body fills its bottom layer first, the emptiest cells first, and drains the top layer first, so every cell of a layer shows the one level. Any cell's handler, bucket or fluid capability is the whole body. A broken cell takes its share; the rest fall apart and form again. An operation that would read an unloaded cell is refused; a body found broken (a cell left while its chunk was away, a structure pasted elsewhere) falls apart and forms again when its cells next tick. Walls, lid, base, ribs and rims show only where the body ends; the liquid is drawn by a block-entity renderer so it runs seamlessly across joined cells.
- **F6.** Sensor Polyp (Bud + comparator + spider eye): reads the fullness of the block it faces every 10 ticks, biomass, then fluid, then items, as 0..15 in its block state, clamped; weak redstone power on every other side and a comparator value; it faces horizontally or down, never up. Owner, 2026-10-20: M3 ships fullness; the other measures come later. Filter Valve (Bone Plate + slime ball + paper): owner, 2026-10-20, built in M3 for the water and restart reserves. It stands on a foundation facing a fluid or biomass store; draining the store through it (a Junction intake, a bucket) stops at the reserve a stick steps through 0, 250, 500, 1000, 2000, 4000, 8000, 16000 mB; filling passes through. Its handler is live and it keeps no block entity.
- **F7.** Fuel Papilla, owner 2026-10-20, the catalog in simple form: a graft (Capillary Gel + Membrane Sheet) used on exposed mature substrate; flush with the ground; a 250 BU buffer that veins and a Junction top up; once a second it fills the chosen worn awakened pieces of one living, non-spectator player standing on it up to the set limit (25, 50, 75 or 100%), 10 BU at most, costing ceil(delivered/5) BU more (`core.equipment.Refuel`). A Filter Valve keeps the source's reserve. It opens a screen; it stays an organ foundation.
