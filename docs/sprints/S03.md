# Sprint S03: living equipment

Milestone: M2, second slice. Status: outline. Full stories are written when S02 is accepted, against the real code.

Goal: the player makes Membrane Sheets and Bone Plates, builds an Awakening Cradle, crafts dormant armor, tools and a weapon, awakens one piece and fills it at the Biomass Bladder.

Acceptance (ROADMAP.md, M2 rest): one awakened armor piece; break/reload preserving one organ history; client models render at all faces and inventory scale.

Decisions already made, so the stories do not reopen them:

- Membrane Rack and Bone Loom reuse the `organ_batch` recipe type and the Bowl's batch code from S02, with `organ` = `membrane_rack` and `bone_loom`. Rack: 1 rotten flesh + 1 string + 100 mB water → 1 Membrane Sheet in 400 ticks; leather instead of flesh takes 800 ticks. Loom: 1 bone + 50 BU → 1 Bone Plate in 400 ticks. Items `membrane_sheet` and `bone_plate` live in a new `construction` module.
- Dormant pieces are vanilla `ArmorItem`s with one `ArmorMaterial` `dormant_flesh` from the Armor Evolution frame table (protection 0/1/1/0, durability 33/48/45/39, toughness 0). Dormant tools are vanilla `DiggerItem`s (pick, hatchet, spade, hoe) with one `Tier` `dormant` at wood-level speed and Bone Plate repair; the Dormant Fang is a `SwordItem`. Recipes follow the Item Catalog rows I013, I015 and I017.
- Living pieces are distinct items (I014, I016, I018) with the same durability and the data component `infestusfrontier:living_equipment` {fuel BU, capacity BU}: 10 BU for hood, legwraps and treads, 40 for the thorax, 20 for tools and the fang, all empty when awakened. Awakening copies the dormant piece's damage. No mutations, counters or symbiosis in this sprint; the component gains fields later.
- The Awakening Cradle is an organ with one service slot and an `organ_batch` recipe per piece: dormant piece + 1 Spore Culture + 1 Membrane Sheet + 100 BU → living piece in 600 ticks. A special recipe class keeps the piece's damage.
- Fill at the Bladder: the service slot accepts any item with `living_equipment`; the Fill button moves min(missing, stored) BU under the `BiomassBuckets.admit` limit and refuses `equipment.full` or `storage.empty`. One transfer per click.
- Equipment models are GeckoLib model stories in ART.md; the platform stories ship vanilla item models with flat placeholders.
