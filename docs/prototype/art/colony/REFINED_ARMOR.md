# Ivory carapace study

The uncommitted (branch 0) suit is the visual quality study. Other mutation variants and the Nutrient Sail are intentionally unchanged. This is a living cuticle, overlapping shell plates, sensory eyes, thoracic organ and vertebral spine—not metal armor with a random material pasted over it. No server behavior changes.

Built-in image generation produced `refined-armor-source.png`; `java tools/ExportColonyAssets.java` mechanically maps its four material quadrants into the 512×256 worn atlases and inventory materials. Geometry is owned by `bioarmor/client/IvoryCarapace.java`. Native pose, trim and glint passes remain in place; custom UV layout is not a promise of exact vanilla trim placement on every added plate.

Exact prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production game texture material atlas for exquisite living insect-inspired armor. A flat square sheet divided into exactly four equal square quadrants with no margins, no borders, no text. Top left: pale warm ivory pearlescent beetle shell, very broad smooth curved growth striations, subtle honey edge tones, no patchwork and no separate scales. Top right: nearly black deep petrol-green flexible organic cuticle, subtle parallel muscle fibers, low contrast. Bottom left: luminous mint-turquoise translucent living sensory membrane, broad central soft light fading to dark teal edges, delicate branching capillaries, no glass highlights or mechanical parts. Bottom right: warm ivory horn with gentle lengthwise ridges, biological growth lines. Every quadrant completely fills its own area edge-to-edge and is a close-up material only, not an object. Beautiful restrained hand-painted stylized game art, clean smooth shapes readable at 64px, even soft lighting, no perspective, no dramatic baked shadow, no grit, no mosaic, no checkerboard, no armor character, no metal, no machinery, no logos. These four surfaces will be assigned separately to modeled plates, flexible joints, sensory organs and ribs.
