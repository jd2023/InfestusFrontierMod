## Final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: seamless tileable game material for a biological biomass reservoir in an original Minecraft mod. Create one square, flat orthographic material swatch filling the whole canvas: pearlescent ivory and very pale blue-green living membrane, broad soft organic tension folds and sparse gently branching capillaries, no square cells or grids. It should suggest a translucent living bladder or insect wing membrane, elegant and readable when reduced to 64x64 pixels. This is a material texture, NOT a rendered object, NOT a scene. Even diffuse lighting; no hard specular hotspots or cast shadows, no border, no central emblem. Use an opaque continuous pale membrane background across the entire image; transparency will be applied by the game renderer, so do not add transparent cutouts or checkerboards. Seamless matching edges. Avoid mechanical edges, pipes, bolts, brickwork, dense grain, text, logos, watermarks. Entire canvas is usable texture.
