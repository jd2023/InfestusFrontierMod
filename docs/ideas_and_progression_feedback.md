## Armor
Armor starts as usual recipe but made with rotten flesh. It provides very little benefits. Just a little of protection or any other benefits. 
First think to do is to "animate" the armor - apply some process, to make it "alive" and useful. Once "alive" it will be able to mend itself (by hurting the player). In order to prevent that we need to add bio storage to the armor. Volume of the storage is one of many parameters that can be mutated in armor. Armor should have a number of counters that tracks activity of the player and adopt. It is one of many ways how armor can be improved. by fusing other materials into the armor it can be made stronger and support more improvements and mutation. Overall budget that can be used on improvement is limited. So we always need to keep in mind our goal and do trade off. Those counters and XP should be possible to extract from the armor and save for future. Or apply saved counters and XP back. 

I need you to work out all ways of evolving the armor.

## Mod specific resources
Initially biomass is all we have and all we need. But we need to have progression in power source and mechanics we use. Here is my proposal on different resources. It should be progressively hard to use more powerful resources. And it should be absolutely necessary to automate everything on previous level to get to a next level.
- biomass - a substance that feeds substances and all organs. It is also fuel the bio armor. 
- DNA material - let me know if it makes any sense, but I think it is important to have someting that will work to measure amount DNA material collected. For that we will have multiple parameters - volume and variety. We need to track particular DNAs that can be used to create mutations, improvements, new organisms. You use some of the DNA material to create these things. Also to extract DNA you need a particular amount of material. We will use drops of the mob (or plant). Basic DNA extractor will need A LOT OF DNA material to extract 100% of a single DNA. Recreate the complete DNA. We will need to figure out how that process will look like exactly and what blocks will we need. Again, everything should be like a puzzle that can be solved in multiple ways. With more power we will be able to create a more powerful and precise DNA extractor. Think about Wither. It will take 100s of Nether Starts to fully analyze its DNA. With a powerful one - just a few. May be we will have one that doesn't even kill the mob, but extracting DNA by inflicting damage slowly.
- Steam - next level. And bio systems do use steam. We will need to also be able to manipulate water and lava. But steam will give us ability to build some more powerful organs. 
- Electricity - definitely bio organs can produce electricity. It would be great to be able to integrate with other mods.
