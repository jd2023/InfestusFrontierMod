# End-to-end gameplay progression

Proposed player projects. SR0–SR9 are guide chapters, not player/base ranks.
Progress depends on actual materials, working organs and dimension-native production.
Initial implementation covers SR0–SR7; SR8–SR9 remain the expansion horizon.
Catalogs own recipes and numerical baselines; this document owns player projects.

## 1. SR0 — Start beside a vanilla home

**Build:** make Spore Culture from ordinary ingredients. Establish selected Living
Substrate, Culture Bowl, Digestive Sac and Biomass Bladder. Prepare membrane and
bone components with Membrane Rack and Bone Loom.

**First loop:** hand-supply permitted organic matter and water to the Sac; store
biomass; spend it on selected ground growth, preparation or equipment. Keep food
and planting stock instead of digesting the whole inventory.

Craft one dormant piece, awaken it in the Cradle, fill it at the Bladder and wear
it during ordinary activity. A first host-grown graft can provide light or armor healing
without a completed creature genome.

**Alternative project:** use Leaching Gland to weaken selected non-ore rock.
See ore through the translucent host, then mine with tools. Leaching does not
pay the ore output or grant Burrowing.

**Recovery:** retain ordinary gear, planting stock and manual feed access.
Remove/feed hungry armor to stop its pre-symbiosis pain. Armor hunger stops at
one heart; enemies, drowning and other environmental damage can still kill.

**Result:** one fed useful piece and a workshop preparing its own starter inputs.
A Sample Pouch collects species-specific death loot without filling ordinary slots.
Carry backup gear: ordinary dropped armor can despawn or be destroyed.

## 2. SR1 — Automate replenishment

```text
Seed Pouch → Planting Proboscis → Cultivation Tissue
                                      ↓
                                Harvest Corolla
                                      ↓
                             Work Bed → Collection Cilia
                                      ↓
                                  filtered split
                         ┌────────────┼─────────────┐
                   seed reserve   player food   surplus feed
                                                    ↓
                                              Digestive Sac
                                                    ↓
                                                Reservoir
                                          ┌─────────┴──────────┐
                                      workshop            Fuel Papilla
```

A Compost Gland uses a separate share of scraps for fertilizer. The same input
cannot produce its full compost and digestion yields. Collection exports held
output; drones and loose item drops are not prerequisites.

**Source choices:** crop beds, managed tree growth and later aquatic habitats.
Manually graft a log into Living Wood to build the first Arbor Root. Convert a
selected tree into mod-native wood and pod-bearing canopy; allocate its growth
among construction wood, pods and sap. Harvest and replant separately. Keep trees
for renewable products or send harvested wood through veins to digestion.

**Circulation:** configure Vascular Junction ports and recessed green veins.
Vascular Stitch crosses exposed terrain steps; a junction joins routes; Septum
Crossing keeps passages isolated. Mount a reservoir above a junction, optionally
raising its stalk to retain a walkway.

**Storage:** mutate Bladders into Reservoir Cells and extend by complete rows/layers.
Invalid additions wait outside the valid body without erasing its contents.
Fuel Papilla fills worn pieces from stored supply.

**Control:** protect seed/restart reserves, then distribute surplus. Full destinations
pause suppliers. Reflex Knot and Selector Ganglion use ready/held/blocked states;
one-tick timing is unnecessary for correctness.

A Dew Gland consumes a share of crops for water. A starter Electrocyte Stack
turns a reserved biomass share into power; charge storage and waste recovery
remain separate jobs. A Carry Sac holds supplies separately from armor.

**Result:** the farm replenishes biomass after its own operating costs. Add demand
and choose more growing area, better recovery, another line or scheduled consumers.

## 3. SR2 — Research a selected specialization

Choose a purpose before collecting: aquatic travel, climbing, a plant trait,
thermal preparation or an organ upgrade. The guide identifies relevant species
and sample/preparation routes.

```text
pouch/collector → raw-sample archive → Specimen Extractor
                                      ├→ genome coverage → DNA Bank
                                      └→ consumable Genetic Stock
complete genome + actual feed → culture additional matching stock
stock + prepared ingredients → Fusion Chrysalis → target-specific graft
graft + existing target → Mutation Chamber → same target, forward mutation
```

Creature DNA comes from death loot, plant DNA from harvested material. A pouch
groups species and quality; a dock unloads it into raw-sample archives. Choose
what to process and which rare samples to reserve for a better extractor.

Store coverage in DNA Banks and attached Archive Lobes, not a player research bar.
A grafting workshop needs access to its physical bank. Complex DNA needs a
Precision Sequencer service; boss/Fold genomes need a Genome Vault. Advanced
grafts combine complete genomes and consume stock from every required species.
Cultured stock cannot be sequenced to manufacture research. A Dissector weapon
trades its combat branch for better sample quality; extractor development and
physical sequencing services improve information recovery independently.

**Armor project:** prepare mineral fusion media and select a permanent branch.
Use the [armor tree](ARMOR_EVOLUTION.md#4-fusion-tree) to plan the piece's specialization.
If unwanted activities fill its shared capacity, pay materials, biomass and your
Minecraft XP at the chamber to remove part of one counter. Branches stay permanent.

**Other projects:** Grafting Bench for plant traits; Feeding Trough plus
Incubation Basket for bounded husbandry; Aquaculture Bed/Fishing Polyp for aquatic
production; Ration Kitchen for meals before surplus is allocated to digestion.

**Mining:** mark a Descending Rootstock job with flush Surveyed Tissue. Supply
access steps, light and output storage. Walk the result to explore a cavern or
address a refused face.

**Mineral workshop:** Grasping Root moves one intact specimen to Work Bed;
Reaction Polyp treats it; Fracture Jaw creates held fragments; Collection Cilia
exports to Mineral Gizzard, Washing Kidney and Bio-Furnace. Hand mining can bypass
treatment. Multiple beds may share an applicator or fracture head.

**Optional courier:** assign a nursery worker to collect held Work Bed results and
deliver them to a capsule along waypoints. One courier can share several separated
beds; fixed cilia/veins offer predictable throughput without pathfinding.

**Result:** a chosen specialization and repeatable preparation, not every genome
or every armor mutation. Helpers do not gate stationary logistics.

## 4. SR3 — Make the Nether the industrial base

Bring trained extractor/vat cores, ordinary construction, planting stock, water,
biomass and a return route. Startup needs no End material or Nether output.

Lay out a Thermal Substrate district before filling it with machines. A Thermal
Root supplies native heat to fungus beds. Separate harvest, water recovery and
digestion organs allocate fruit among water, biomass, membranes, culture feed and
nursery lining. Reserve the parent fungi and the next operating cycle's supplies.

Grow the first lining beside a magma block, then build a native Thermal Mantle
around the existing Bio-Furnace. Make tempered plates and upgrade the same
Electrocyte core into industrial conversion. Cooling, wastewater recovery,
charge storage and cutoff controls remain separate construction projects.

This base grows **larger and more productive than the Overworld workshop**.
Allocate one line to upkeep, another to refining and a third to exports.
Adding consumers before feed/cooling is ready drains storage; protected reserves
allow a restart without another Overworld fuel delivery. Native fruit supplies
membranes and culture feed, not minerals, creature DNA or every farming specialty.

Install developed extractors/vats on native thermal bodies. Nether species require
this actual dimension even if killed elsewhere. An Overworld mob farm cannot
remove the need for a Nether laboratory and continuing local stock culture.

**Mining choices:** a Descending Cradle advances a supplied shaft workstation;
Boring Jaw serves a real cutting mount; Digestion Crucible treats a contained pit
while leaving ore recovery separate. Plan access, spoil handling and fuel before
starting each finite section.

**Result:** local production replaces its own water, biomass and power consumption,
makes continuing thermal products and exports useful surplus to the Overworld.

## 5. SR4 — Choose how much recovery to pay for

The developed Electrocyte Stack powers industrial loads; Charge Sac buffers it.
Conductive Tissue carries it. Precision Sequencer and Ion Separator perform their
listed preparation tasks, not every earlier operation.

Improve [ore recovery and smelting](ITEM_CATALOG.md#ore-dust-and-recovery)
independently; stockpile compatible dust for a later smelter.

**Layouts:** direct smelting, partial treatment plus stockpiling, parallel specialist
lines or shared treatment equipment. Compare energy per ingot, ingots per time,
construction material and occupied space. Maximum recovery must have costs on
other measures rather than dominate every route.

Sensors and Scheduler Ganglion control finite jobs. Display Membrane reports
full stores, protected reserves and blocked work. Byproducts have held outputs
and an available same-stage disposal route. Pressure/heat faults can rupture a
vessel: leaked biomass kills unsealed substrate and can destroy organs. Build a
sealed catchment, recovery path and cutoff around hazardous processes. Ordinary
full outputs still pause; failure handling belongs in the factory layout.

Bank player XP in a Mnemonic Vessel and supply counter-reduction chambers from it.
Keep a reserve for vanilla enchanting instead of consuming every point in rituals.

Meet the extractor's practice gate, install sequencing services and supply its
precision batches. Preview coverage before consuming boss samples. DNA recovery,
ore recovery and smelting efficiency compete for the same industrial supplies.

**Result:** a chosen refinery, an improved lab and a demonstrated interruption
recovery path.

## 6. SR5 — Build an End settlement

Bring return supplies, biomass, thermal products, electricity support and
construction stock. Ordinary blocks provide first shelter.

Grow Anchored Substrate and Anchor Roots around Spatial Nursery. Earlier
membranes, Nether Thermal Lining, chorus fruit, biomass and electricity produce
Spatial Membranes. Spatial Conditioner prepares their passenger, cargo or precision
use. Native production stays in the End.

Chorus Orchard Tissue grows local feed. Divide fruit among water, digestion,
membranes and nursery inputs. Native cultivation needs a powered resonator and
anchored footprint; its longer cycle requires a larger restart buffer. Imports
start the site, not its permanent fuel supply. Separate cultivation and membrane
conditioning rooms so retuning one cannot silently stop the other.

Develop extractor/vat bodies for End-native shulker/dragon research and continuing
stock culture. First shulker DNA uses earlier sequencing services; levitation-
assisted precision is a later improvement, not its own bootstrap requirement.

Spatial preparations also support selected permanent armor branches. Flight needs
an actual Elytra; underground travel needs fuel, stability, visibility and air.
A spatial ingredient does not automatically provide all of these.

**Result:** repeated End production supplying equipment, freight and synthesis.
The dragon encounter does not substitute for this workshop.

## 7. SR6 — Connect three productive sites

| Site | Local catalog work | Export | Imports/support |
|---|---|---|---|
| Overworld agriculture | Food, ordinary crops, wood, diverse stock | Meals, specialist farm products | Industrial surplus and thermal/spatial products |
| Nether industrial base | Renewable local biomass/power, thermal manufacturing, Nether DNA, bulk refining | Biomass/energy surplus, lining, plates, refined materials | Specialist crops and spatial products |
| End spatial settlement | Powered native cultivation, local biomass/power, End DNA and conditioning | Conditioned membranes and spatial stock | Thermal products and specialist prepared materials |

Transit Maw/Arrival Chamber move passengers. Freight Gullet/Cargo Locks move
reserved real item/fluid batches. They may share one settlement gateway layout.
A spatially upgraded Sail Roost can keep a selected chunk ticking, consuming
biomass continuously. Fit critical organs inside its previewed boundary and retain
local restart fuel; remote travel and freight never enable loading automatically.

**Choose:** separate local production lines, shared local services or buffered
shipment schedules. Each dimension sustains its own baseline fuel/power.
Storage Cortex indexes local physical stores;
remote availability and freight remain separate, not an instantly spendable global tank.

**Unloading:** ordinary freight waits with accounted cargo until both endpoints
are available. Remote farms do not run invisibly. Freight does not load chunks.

A Recall Nest introduces costly per-piece equipment recovery. Cocoon → Recall
or Death Bond is a permanent armor decision, paid separately for each piece;
a backpack is not protected with it. An unloaded recall berth means fallback,
not free chunk loading.

**Result:** make a useful product from all three sites' contributions. Preserve
restart reserves so staged visits and shipments work without permanently loaded worlds.

## 8. SR7 — Scale production and strategic reserves

Supply parallel processing lines from farms and native products. Protect seeds,
meals, research stock and emergency fuel from bulk consumers. Develop individual
organs through actual completed work; there is no automatic district-wide bonus.

Synthesis Heart, Anatomy Sockets and Catalyst Lobes combine installed operations.
Expression Switch selects supported organ process profiles, never armor branches.

A reservoir district contains several finite bodies, isolation valves, production
buffers, export buffers and a protected reserve. Capacity and throughput are
separate. Display reserve endurance at current net drain.

**Completion projects:** a traversable deep mine feeding a selected ore route;
an expedition/refueling network; a supplied specialist equipment workshop; or
production meeting a specified energy/space/output target. Completing every
optional catalog entry is not assumed.

## 9. SR8–SR9 — Fold and Manyfold horizon

The proposed Fold has native feeding and hardening phases. Bring ordinary shelter
and a return provision. Establish a local bed and Founder Nursery for Adaptive Gel
without boss loot.

**Layouts:** buffer products grown during favorable natural phases, or supply
Habitat Lung/Retuning Root support for a bounded working habitat. Foldroot,
Pulse Reed and Glassbloom have different outputs. Phase Collector gathers native
charge; Phase Accumulator stores it. Retuning cannot generate its own consumed charge.

Adaptive Culture Loom combines thermal, spatial and native products. Equipment
presets change settings within installed anatomy, not a hidden second loadout.

Manyfold is player-initiated at a prepared site: observe its state → supply a
countercondition → attack during the opening. Solo preparation must work.
Failure cannot destroy the three-world colony. Boss death samples enter native
research; complete reconstruction is an efficiency project, not a guaranteed
first-victory reward. Once complete, culturing supplies additional Genetic Stock
without producing trophies or ordinary boss loot.

## 10. Implementation milestones

These are delivery checkpoints, not player tiers. Each ends in a playable build.
Implement its rules, recipes, discovery, UI, saves, original assets and diagnostics
together. Do not defer every visual or usability check to the final milestone.
All listed branches are implemented across the sequence; players choose which
ones to pursue. Fold Gateway and all Fold-dependent recipes/grafts remain outside
M0–M10 regardless of their catalog grouping.

| Milestone | Depends on | Playable result | Owning gameplay modules |
|---|---|---|---|
| M0 — First-slice foundation | Bootstrap | Reproducible client/server launch; approved library pins; one real organ screen driven by a tiny tested UI module and server-validated intents | UI foundation, platform adapters, bounded simulation |
| M1 — Feed a living workshop | M0 | Start in Survival, discover culture, grow selected ground, digest feed, store biomass, prepare materials and awaken/feed one piece | Ecology, biological processing, storage, equipment, discovery |
| M2 — Self-supplying Overworld | M1 | Crops/trees remain useful living scenery; separate growers, harvesters, collectors, veins and reserves supply water/biomass/starter power; refuel at mutated ground | Cultivation, logistics, control, equipment service |
| M3 — First designed trait | M2 | Pouch → dock/archive → extractor → bank → cultured stock → multi-species graft; separate Carry Sac; choose combat or Dissector weapon | Genetics, portable storage, equipment evolution |
| M4 — Specialist equipment and manual industry | M3 | Independent armor counters/caps, forward fusion, paid partial clearing, hunger/symbiosis, dye/trims/enchantments; manual leaching and separately controlled ore treatment/fracture/smelting | Equipment, mineral processing, excavation |
| M5 — Nether industrial settlement | M4 | Native heat/crops/water, industrial power/cooling, thermal materials and local DNA/stock production support sustained exports; thermal armor provides bounded work windows | Native cultivation, thermal processing, genetics, equipment |
| M6 — Precision and major excavation | M5 | Independent ore/smelting improvement paths, R2 sequencing, learned organ upgrades, shafts/descending tunnels/strips, controlled hazardous processes and recovery | Processing, excavation, process control, containment |
| M7 — Independent End settlement | M6 | Local powered chorus cultivation supports native biomass/power, shulker research, spatial conditioning and R3 sequencing; late burrowing/flight branches become usable | Spatial processing, genetics, equipment movement |
| M8 — One distributed colony | M7 | Passenger and real-cargo links join all three bases; storage indexing and workshop requests use actual organs; per-piece Cocoon/Recall/Death Bond function | Transit, logistics, storage indexing, equipment preservation |
| M9 — Complete production choices | M8 | Remaining SR0–SR7 catalog branches: aquatic/husbandry/food/potion routes, helper professions, defensive organs, paid Sail loading, synthesis and finite reservoir districts | Existing feature owners; no catch-all endgame module |
| M10 — Complete campaign gate | M9 | Fresh-world Survival route reaches three-world synthesis; alternate layouts and equipment lineages remain viable; remaining supported armor ranks and catalog recipes are verified | Integration and balance across existing modules |

M0 evaluates UI/guide/animation libraries against the actual first screen and
asset; it does not create unused frameworks. Exact artifacts, required/optional
status and absence/presence tests are fixed before their feature is built.
JEI discovery is essential, advancements remain, FTB Quests is not shipped.
Separate content mods and backpacks cannot be required for the core loop.

### Acceptance at each checkpoint

| Checkpoint | Required evidence beyond the authoritative full gate |
|---|---|
| M0 | Client screen capture, headless dedicated-server launch, invalid/stale command rejection; UI internals cannot import gameplay policy |
| M1 | Fresh Survival acquisition and recipe reveal, empty/full inventory, insufficient biomass, break/reload preserving one organ history; client models render at all faces and inventory scale |
| M2 | Three complete reference supply cycles with a positive measured surplus; seed/water/restart reserves retained; 3D branches/crossings and port direction inspected visually; full outputs and unloaded routes cannot spill or duplicate |
| M3 | All sample qualities and capacities, last-slot contention, no-drop vanilla species, duplicate death callbacks, relocation across dimensions, copied-record merge, consumed sample never recreated; no ordinary-slot pollution or nested pack duplication |
| M4 | Every activity has one owner, sum-cap clipping, partial clearing costs, permanent branch rejection, one-heart hunger floor, sleep/rescue persistence; ore conservation against direct mining/Fortune and retained dust |
| M5 | Start with the declared finite kit, replace every recurring fuel/water input locally, manufacture/export lining, then restart without Overworld fuel; wrong dimension/bed/level/mutation each independently refuses unchanged |
| M6 | Walk every excavation type including descent, turn, section continuation and light/access; cavities/fluids/protected blocks/output-full/chunk boundary stop safely; bounded spill containment tested under shared load |
| M7 | First End materials need no shulker genome; first shulker genome needs no R3 service; local powered cultivation restarts from reserve; burrowing cannot fly or erase terrain, and wing fuel exhaustion leaves the specified fallback |
| M8 | Concurrent freight, crash/restart mid-transfer, both endpoint unloads, changed ownership, unsafe destination and full stores; each preserved armor piece has exactly one owner after death/respawn; no unrequested chunk tickets |
| M9 | Catalog-to-registry/recipe/guide coverage for every in-scope entry; helpers obey population/path budgets and release dead reservations; connected storage renders without internal seams; optional-mod and no-content-mod profiles |
| M10 | Fresh-world playthrough plus a second materially different layout; packaged dedicated server with two clients; worst-case shared-budget soak at declared limits; restart/save recovery, dense-base TPS/FPS profiles and human appearance/usability review |

Before implementing a milestone, split it into narrow tasks using the existing
task template. Each task names its owner, concrete inputs/outputs, finite work and
state budgets, rollback/refusal behavior and failing acceptance tests. Checkpoint,
test, review and commit each coherent scope; no whole-catalog code dump.

The full gate grows with implementation: recipe/data checks, pure rules,
GameTests, client fixtures and packaged compatibility profiles. A documentation
calculation is not a gameplay test. Record measured throughput and resource
consumption from reference builds, then tune owning recipe tables. Compare
productivity, footprint and supply cost; do not improve all three for every route.

### Expansion after the three-world campaign

Fold development follows a separately playable foothold → native sustainable
habitat → adaptive processing → Manyfold encounter → reconstructed boss stock
sequence. Its first habitat cannot require boss loot. Attack waves, organ-eating
hostiles and village transformation remain outside these milestones.
