# Roadmap

“Implemented” means the behavior exists in the current code. It does not mean Survival balance or multiplayer play has been accepted. Unchecked content is planned.

## M1 — Grow the colony's ground and walls

**Status:** Implemented.

**Content:**
- [x] Spore Culture, Living Substrate and its maturity stages; bone meal accelerates maturity.
- [x] Living walls, reinforcement grafts, Membrane Window and Sphincter Door.
- [x] Conversion of vegetation and selected trees into withered plants, infected logs and blighted leaves.
- [x] The Waking Genome guide and starter advancements.

**Steps:**
1. Craft Spore Culture beside an ordinary Minecraft home.
2. Choose the ground to convert. Mature the substrate before building organs on it.
3. Extend the footprint through player-directed growth with bounded server work.
4. Grow walls and add openings to make a usable workshop.

**Finish:** The player can establish and expand a colony footprint using obtainable materials and the in-game guide.

## M2 — Feed a workshop and make equipment

**Status:** Workshop and equipment implemented. Growing organs from simpler living forms remains incomplete.

**Content:**
- [x] Culture Bowl, Digestive Sac, Biomass Bladder and Organ Bud.
- [x] Membrane Rack, Bone Loom, Awakening Cradle and Bio-Furnace.
- [x] Dormant and awakened helmet, chestplate, leggings and boots.
- [x] Dormant and living pick, hatchet, spade, hoe and fang weapon.
- [x] Synaptic Probe diagnostics, workshop recipes, guide entries and advancements.
- [ ] Grow workshop organs from living buds using prepared materials; retain crafting for starter ingredients.

**Steps:**
1. Supply organic matter and water to the Digestive Sac. Store the resulting biomass.
2. Use the Bowl, Rack and Loom to prepare materials, then feed them into living buds to grow workshop organs.
3. Smelt useful goods in the Bio-Furnace, spending biomass instead of furnace fuel.
4. Craft a dormant armor piece, awaken it in the Cradle and fill it from the Bladder.
5. Use the probe to identify missing inputs or blocked outputs.

**Finish:** Grow and supply a working workshop from obtainable starter materials, smelt useful goods and awaken one equipment piece.

## M3 — Keep the farm and workshop supplied

**Status:** Core content implemented; the connected Survival route remains to be completed.

**Content:**
- [x] Activation Cyst and preparation treatments.
- [x] Cultivation Tissue, Seed Pouch, Planting Proboscis, Harvest Corolla and Compost Gland.
- [x] Biomass, Fluid and Item Veins; Vascular Junction, Septum Crossing and Vascular Stitch.
- [x] Mouths, Feeding Lobes and Yield Lobes for moving items into and out of stores and organs.
- [x] Item Capsule, Capsule Stem, Biomass Reservoir, Filter Valve and Sensor Polyp.
- [x] Fuel Papilla for refilling worn equipment at the base.

**Steps:**
1. Build a wheat plot. Supply seeds to the Proboscis and collect the Corolla's harvest.
2. Keep planting stock and player food. Send only surplus crops to digestion; allocate separate scraps to compost.
3. Use a Junction and Fluid Veins to supply water from a renewable vanilla source.
4. Connect biomass production, storage and consumers. Set Mouth filters, amounts and priorities.
5. Add furnace demand. Increase farm output or reduce consumption if biomass falls.
6. Keep enough seeds, water and biomass to restart after an interruption.

**Finish:** The farm replenishes seeds, water and biomass while supplying the furnace. It resumes after a supply interruption without manual restocking.

## M3a — Choose the first evolution branches

**Status:** Planned. Existing organ stages and equipment grafts do not provide this branching system.

**Content:**
- [ ] Experience from completed work for the Bio-Furnace and Living Pick.
- [ ] Two permanent specializations for each: stronger basic operation or support for more complex mutations.

**Steps:**
1. Smelt with the furnace and mine with the pick to earn experience.
2. Inspect each branch's benefits and limits before choosing.
3. Evolve each independently. Continue using it along the chosen branch.

**Finish:** Both have useful, distinct branches. Choices survive relocation and saves and cannot be reset or switched.

## M3b — Turn mined ore into crafted products

**Status:** Implemented; Survival play acceptance remains.

**Content:**
- [x] Mixed Craw for mixed storage; Larder with labels and reserves protected from automatic extraction.
- [x] Mineral Gizzard, iron/gold/copper dust, Tailings and Rock Residue.
- [x] Washing Kidney, dirty-water storage and settling.
- [x] Shaping Palm: retained crafting grid, access to connected stores, and repetition of one selected recipe.
- [x] Connected iron-bars factory in the demo and an automated factory test.

**Steps:**
1. Mine raw iron, gold or copper with ordinary tools. Put it in a Larder and reserve the stock the factory must not use.
2. Grind each raw item into four dust plus waste.
3. Optionally wash its Tailings for one additional dust. Supply water and biomass, and settle dirty water into residue.
4. Smelt four dust into one ingot in the Bio-Furnace. Store leftovers for the next batch.
5. Craft manually at a Palm. Apply Contractile Fiber for access through the Mouth beneath it and connected Item Veins.
6. Apply Synaptic Gel for Rote, select a recipe, and supply ingredients and biomass. The Palm repeats that recipe and holds outputs until they fit.
7. Route ingots into the iron-bars recipe and collect bars. Provide separate waste storage or disposal.

**Finish:** Supplied ore becomes iron bars automatically. Full product or waste storage pauses production; clearing space resumes it.

## M3c — Expand the Mineral Gizzard

**Status:** Planned.

**Content:**
- [ ] A multiblock Gizzard with working chambers and separate input, product and waste connections.
- [ ] Dismantling and rebuilding around the original core.

**Steps:**
1. Supply parts and grow an expanded body around an existing Gizzard.
2. Connect ore, biomass, dust and waste routes.
3. Increase supply to use the extra processing capacity.
4. Dismantle and rearrange the body when the layout needs changing.

**Finish:** The expanded Gizzard handles more work with matching supply and output capacity. Rebuilding preserves the core's identity, experience and evolution branch.

## M4 — Produce and apply genetic constructs

**Status:** Planned.

**Content:**
- [ ] Sample collection, Specimen Extractor, DNA Bank and Genetic Culture Vat.
- [ ] Species stock, genetic constructs, Fusion Chrysalis and Mutation Chamber.
- [ ] One single-species mutation and one mutation combining two species.

**Steps:**
1. Collect DNA samples and complete the required species genomes.
2. Use completed genomes and supplied biological material to produce species stock.
3. Produce a mutation's genetic construct from the required stock.
4. Supply the construct and energy to mutate a compatible organ or equipment piece.

**Finish:** Both mutations add their stated capabilities. Each application consumes a construct and energy; completed genome records remain available.

## M5 — Specialize equipment and treat rock

**Status:** Planned beyond the basic equipment shipped in M2.

**Content:**
- [ ] Extend work-earned experience and permanent evolution branches to each armor piece and remaining living tools.
- [ ] Fuel-consuming abilities, healing, hunger and recovery behavior.
- [ ] Leaching Gland and Leached Rock.
- [ ] Work Bed, Grasping Root, Reaction Polyp, Fracture Jaw and Collection Cilia.

**Steps:**
1. Earn experience with each piece and choose its permanent specialization.
2. Produce genetic constructs for compatible abilities. Apply them with energy and supply their operating costs.
3. Leach selected non-ore rock to expose ore, then mine it by hand.
4. Build a treatment station: move an intact specimen onto a Work Bed, treat it, fracture it and export held fragments to the mineral workshop.

**Finish:** A specialized piece provides its stated ability and cost. The treatment route produces a measured yield without duplicating the original ore. Hand mining remains usable.

## M6 — Build a Nether base that supplies itself

**Status:** Planned.

**Content:**
- [ ] Thermal Substrate, Thermal Root, Thermal Cultivation Tissue and Thermal Nursery.
- [ ] Thermal Lining, Thermal Mantle and thermal manufacturing.
- [ ] Lava Siphon, Steam Heart, Steam Vein, Pressure Vesicle, Relief Chimney and Condenser.
- [ ] Thermal body expansions for existing extractors and culture vats; industrial power conversion.

**Steps:**
1. Bring a finite startup kit: construction stock, planting stock, water, biomass and a return route.
2. Grow native fungi and split their products between water recovery, digestion, membranes and culture feed.
3. Produce the first lining beside magma. Build a Thermal Mantle around a Bio-Furnace and manufacture thermal products.
4. Add heat, steam and power equipment with cooling, pressure relief and stored restart supplies.
5. Research Nether species locally and export surplus lining and refined materials.

**Finish:** Replace recurring fuel and water locally, export useful products, and restart after a shutdown without an Overworld fuel delivery.

## M7 — Improve recovery and excavate real tunnels

**Status:** Planned.

**Content:**
- [ ] Electrocyte Stack, Conductive Tissue, Charge Sac, Ion Separator and Precision Sequencer.
- [ ] Descending Rootstock, Surveyed Tissue, Descending Cradle, Boring Jaw and Strata Maw.
- [ ] Spoil Sorter, Recovery Sump, Neutralization Gland and Pressure Regulator.
- [ ] Scheduler Ganglion and Display Membrane.

**Steps:**
1. Supply power and compare direct smelting with washing and separation. Choose a yield that justifies its energy, space and waste costs.
2. Add precision sequencing to existing research equipment and expand processing bodies for the increased throughput.
3. Mark a finite excavation section. Supply its cutting equipment, access steps, light and output storage.
4. Walk the resulting tunnel. Resolve fluids, protected blocks or full outputs before continuing.
5. Add containment, recovery and cutoff controls around hazardous processes.

**Finish:** Excavation removes real blocks and leaves accessible passages. Jobs stop safely at unavailable chunks and obstructions. A refinery can recover from interruption without losing its accounted inputs or overflowing waste.

- [ ] Chorus Resonator, Spatial Membranes and Spatial Conditioner.

**Steps:**

## M9 — Connect the three bases

**Status:** Planned.

**Content:**
- [ ] Transit Maw and Arrival Chamber for passengers.
- [ ] Freight Gullet and Cargo Lock for item and fluid shipments.
- [ ] Storage Cortex, paid Sail Roost chunk loading and Recall Nest equipment recovery.

**Steps:**
1. Build safe arrival points and link the bases for travel.
2. Reserve shipment batches and send thermal, agricultural and spatial products between sites.
3. Index local stores; keep remote shipments separate from locally available stock.
4. Add a spatial mutation to a Sail Roost and supply its running cost to keep a selected chunk active.
5. Choose and fund a permanent recovery branch separately for each armor piece.

**Finish:** Make a product using all three bases. Freight survives interruption with one accounted owner per batch. Travel and freight do not load chunks automatically; each base retains local restart supplies.

**Status:** Partly implemented: Spine Sentry and Aerocyte Bloom exist. The production and helper branches below remain planned.

**Content:**
- [x] Spine Sentry and Aerocyte Bloom, with supplied attacks and bounded target searches.
- [ ] Arbor Root, Living Wood, Canopy Cyst and Sap Tap for managed wood, pods and sap production.
- [ ] Aquaculture Bed and Fishing Polyp; Feeding Trough, Milking Lobe and Incubation Basket.
- [ ] Ration Kitchen and Brewing Gland.
- [ ] Brood Nursery, Worker Waypoint and courier helpers.
- [ ] Synthesis Heart, Anatomy Sockets, Catalyst Lobes and Expression Switch.

**Steps:**
1. Add tree, aquatic or animal production to supply a chosen material. Existing tree infection does not provide these renewable production systems.
2. Reserve breeding stock and player meals before sending surplus to industry.
3. Assign optional couriers between separated workstations; retain fixed veins as an alternative.
4. Add specialized mutations to existing defenses and supply them without draining farm reserves.
5. Expand production into synthesis bodies with Anatomy Sockets and Catalyst Lobes; supply each installed operation.

**Finish:** Each branch replenishes its inputs or clearly consumes an external supply. Helpers respect population and path limits. Defense and synthesis cannot silently exhaust protected reserves.

## M11 — Complete the campaign in Survival

**Status:** Not complete.

**Content:** No new progression system. Finish recipes, guide coverage, balance, recovery and multiplayer behavior across M1–M10.

**Steps:**
1. Start a fresh world and follow the guide from Spore Culture to three connected productive bases.
2. Build a second materially different factory layout and compare its supply and waste requirements.
3. Repeat shared production and travel on a dedicated server with two players.
4. Run a dense colony, interrupt supplies, unload bases and restart the server.

**Finish:** Complete the three-base campaign with usable instructions, sustainable supply chains and stable saves and performance. The Fold dimension and its boss remain outside this campaign.
