# Infestus Frontier

A player-controlled living colony spanning the Overworld, Nether and End.
Progress comes from biological preparation, DNA research, specialized equipment
and interconnected production—not repetitive harvesting.

- Expand only into selected ground with finite supplies. Keep the colony varied, alive and useful, including its vegetation.
- Build from reusable organs with separate jobs. Automation rewards layout, resource allocation and reliable sequencing.
- Reconstruct creature DNA from species-specific death loot; improve sample quality and native laboratories. Complex mutations combine multiple complete genomes and consume their physical stock.
- Grow and reinforce existing organs. Preserve their development when moved.
- Evolve each armor piece independently along permanent branches. Specialized equipment outperforms universal equipment in its chosen role.
- Mine real terrain. Excavations remain traversable; Burrowing crosses ground without extracting resources.
- Establish local renewable biomass and power in every dimension. The Overworld is the starter/agricultural base; the Nether becomes the larger industrial base, and the End develops native spatial industry. Connections carry both players and materials, not permanent Overworld fuel dependence.
- Keep early machinery useful through alternative production routes. Later advancement must remain harder even with better technology; it requires automation, coordinated supply chains and increasing resources, not repeated manual grinding.
- Preserve vanilla relevance, advancements and an in-game guide. FTB Quests remains a modpack responsibility.
- Use original biological names and art: muscle, bone, membranes, visible fluids and living ground cover.
- Bound simulation, storage and output. No unbounded drops or implicit chunk loading.
- Deliver the Overworld–Nether–End game in playable increments, then tune through play. A fourth dimension and boss are long-term scope.
- Attack waves, organ-seeking hostiles and village transformation are deferred. A separate backpack provides storage, not automatic armor abilities.

Recipes, numerical balance and stage assignments in the gameplay catalogs are proposed design.
Unresolved decisions belong in [Open questions](docs/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md).
